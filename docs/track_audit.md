# TRACK Audit

The TRACK audit framework in the Ujima prototype includes:

- Training Data Audit: review sample distributions and representation in the training dataset.
- Representation Audit: compare applicant county and occupation rates against a baseline.
- Amplification Detection: identify whether loan shark or school fee signals increase risk polarity.
- Counterfactual Testing: evaluate how outcomes change when occupation shifts from Market Vendor to Formal Employee.
- Kill Switch Evaluation: support explicit override and human takeover through guardrail activation.

TRACK results are surfaced in the dashboard to show bias findings, counterfactual differences, and safety assessment details.
